GoldBod Financial Statement Analysis

Financial analysis of Ghana Gold Board, GoldBod, covering its first full audited year, FY2025, against FY2024 comparatives from its predecessor, PMMC. Built in response to IMF and local media scrutiny of GoldBod's reported surplus and the Domestic Gold Purchase Programme, DGPP, transfer risk.

What's in this repo

GoldBod_Financial_Analysis.xlsx, formula-verified workbook with income statement, balance sheet, and key ratios
etl.py, Python script converting the workbook into tidy CSVs for Power BI
GoldBod_Dashboard.pbix, 7-page Power BI dashboard
GoldBod_Industry_Operations_Analysis.pdf, boardroom-grade write-up covering industry context, operations, financial performance, risk, and recommendations

Key findings

Reported surplus GH₵5.46 billion, but 81.88% is government seed capital. Core operating surplus from actual fee business: GH₵909.7 million.
Total expenditure fell 15.5% while headcount grew 294.7%, cutting cost per employee 78.6%.
Cash grew 1,088%, total assets grew 476.4%, liabilities-to-assets ratio improved from 92.4% to 41.70%.
GoldBod's reported surplus and the IMF's GH₵22 billion cited DGPP loss measure different things, one is fee income, the other is Bank of Ghana's gold-trading losses under a program GoldBod absorbed in July 2026.
Stress-test scenarios model what DGPP-scale losses could mean for GoldBod's own balance sheet post-transfer.
